# Attribution and Content Policy

All lesson text, figures, and code in this course are original to the course and were written specifically for it. Every concept is explained in our own words.

The textbook and research papers named on the [References](references.md) page are cited as recommended reading. They are **not** reproduced here in any form: we do not copy or closely paraphrase their prose, figures, captions, equations-as-written, or problem statements. Citations point you to each source so you can consult the primary treatment directly.

In particular, J. N. Kutz's *Data-Driven Modeling & Scientific Computation* is the textbook that inspired the scope and sequence of this course. If you want the authoritative, in-depth treatment of these methods, please **obtain Kutz's textbook separately** through your library or a bookseller; this course is a companion, not a substitute.
